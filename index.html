How I'm Reading The Sports Analytics Market

What I'm watching and focusing on as I move into sports analytics and how it lines up with the skills above.

  • 01

    Front offices are hiring for data fluency, not just sport fluency

    Analytics roles increasingly favor candidates who can pair statistical modeling with clear communication. The exact combination I built through econometrics coursework and currently in my MSBA program.

  • 02

    Teams are building analytics in-house insted of outsourcing.

    Front offices and league offices are building dedicated analytics departments rather than contracting the work outwards, creating exactly the kind of full-time analyst roles I'm targeting.

  • 03

    Probabilistic thinking is a baseline skill

    From fantasy roster construction to in-game win probability, sports analytics runs on the same equity and expected-value thinking I use studying poker strategy and fantasy sports.

  • 04

    Fan engagement is now a data product

    With ticketing, betting, and media partners treating fan behavior as a high-value dataset, sports analytics now spans both the front office and the back office. My current focus is applying advanced analytics to team and player operations, but I am equally energized by how data drives the modern sports business as a whole.
